Scam call player

Open this on a laptop. It plays the "scammer" out loud for the iPhone to hear.

How to demo

  1. On the iPhone, open Safe Signal, pick a caller ID, tap Simulate incoming call and Accept.
  2. Hold the iPhone near this laptop's speaker and press a script below.
  3. After the alert, tap Let T-Mobile handle it. The voice is saved.
  4. Call again with a different caller ID and play another script with the same voice. It gets recognized.
